# Taiwan Labor & Employment Framework Reference

Detailed Taiwan labor law working summary for employment contract drafting and review. Use this file for issue-spotting and structured thinking, not as the final citation authority when an official source can verify the point.

## How to Read This File

- Treat this file as a **secondary reference**.
- For statute names, article numbers, current law status, and mandatory legal requirements, verify against `law.moj.gov.tw` before presenting the point as definite.
- If this file conflicts with the official source, the official source wins.

- Sections **1-5** are primarily statute-grounded legal rules.
- Sections **6-8** mix legal framing with drafting heuristics and review checklists.
- When writing findings, distinguish:
  - **法律要求 / statutory baseline**
  - **可執行性風險 / enforceability risk**
  - **實務偏好 / drafting or market preference**

If the user asks for the **latest** legal or regulatory status, verify current law before treating any time-sensitive note here as current.

## 1. Labor Standards Act

PCode: N0030001

The baseline statute for all employment relationships in Taiwan. Most provisions are mandatory minimum standards — contracts may provide more favorable terms to the employee but cannot derogate below the statutory floor.

### §2 — Definitions

- **雇主 (Employer)**: business owner, business management agent, or person exercising authority on behalf of the employer over the worker
- **勞工 (Worker/Employee)**: person employed to perform work for wages
- **工資 (Wages)**: all regular compensation received for work performed, regardless of name — includes base salary, fixed allowances, and any regularly paid items. Excludes year-end bonuses, hazard pay, and travel allowances unless paid regularly.

The classification matters: if the relationship involves direction, supervision, and organizational subordination, it is likely an employment relationship subject to the LSA, even if the contract says "承攬" or "委任."

### §9 — Employment Contract Types

| Type | Duration | Termination |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **不定期契約 (Indefinite-term)** | Continuous work — the default for most employment | Employer must have statutory grounds (§11 or §12) to terminate |
| **定期契約 (Fixed-term)** | Temporary, short-term, seasonal, or specific-task work | Expires at term end; auto-converts to indefinite if worker continues working after expiry without objection |

**Key rule**: Continuous or permanent work must use indefinite-term contracts. Using repeated fixed-term contracts for continuous work risks being deemed indefinite-term by courts, with full termination protection applying.

### §21 — Minimum Wage

Wages cannot fall below the statutory minimum wage (基本工資). Updated annually by the Ministry of Labor.

- Monthly minimum wage: set by administrative order (check current year)
- Hourly minimum wage: set by administrative order
- Applies to all workers under the LSA regardless of contract terms

### §24 — Overtime Pay Rates

| Overtime hours | Rate |
|---------------|------|
| First 2 hours beyond normal working hours | Base hourly rate × 1⅓ (加給 1/3) |
| Beyond 2 hours | Base hourly rate × 1⅔ (加給 2/3) |
| Rest day (§36 休息日) — first 2 hours | Base hourly rate × 1⅓ |
| Rest day — beyond 2 hours (up to 8) | Base hourly rate × 1⅔ |
| Rest day — beyond 8 hours | Base hourly rate × 2⅔ |
| National holiday or regular leave day | Base hourly rate × 2 (doubled) |

Overtime calculation basis is the "normal hourly rate" derived from the monthly wage ÷ 30 ÷ 8 (for monthly-salaried workers).

### §30 — Normal Working Hours

- Daily: max **8 hours**
- Weekly: max **40 hours**
- 2-week/4-week/8-week flexible working hour systems (§30-1, §30-2) allow redistribution with caps
- Employer and employee may agree to shift the 8-hour allocation under flexible systems, but total weekly/period caps remain

### §32 — Overtime Caps

- Monthly overtime: max **46 hours** (general rule)
- With labor-management meeting agreement or union consent: up to **54 hours/month**, but total in any 3-month period cannot exceed **138 hours**
- Certain industries may apply for further exemptions with MOL approval

### §36 — Rest Days (例假日 + 休息日)

Every 7 days, the worker is entitled to:
- 1 **例假日** (mandatory rest day — work only for force majeure)
- 1 **休息日** (rest day — work allowed with consent + premium pay)

Standard implementation: 2-day weekend, but allocation is flexible under collective agreements.

### §37 — National Holidays (國定假日)

Workers have paid leave on designated national holidays (currently ~12 days/year). If work is required, the employer must pay double wages or provide a compensatory day off.

### §38 — Annual Paid Leave (特別休假)

| Service period | Annual leave days |
|---------------|-------------------|
| 6 months – 1 year | 3 days |
| 1 – 2 years | 7 days |
| 2 – 3 years | 10 days |
| 3 – 5 years | 14 days |
| 5 – 10 years | 15 days |
| 10+ years | +1 day per year, max 30 days |

- Leave scheduling: primarily at the employee's discretion, coordinated with the employer
- Unused leave: must be paid out at year-end or upon separation, at the worker's current daily wage rate
- Employers must notify employees of their accrued leave at the beginning of each year

### §43 — Other Leave Types

The LSA itself is thin on leave types beyond annual leave. Most leave rules come from the **勞工請假規則** (Worker Leave Regulations, N0030006):

| Leave type | Duration | Pay |
|-----------|----------|-----|
| 婚假 (Marriage) | 8 days | Full pay |
| 喪假 (Bereavement) | 3-8 days depending on relation | Full pay |
| 事假 (Personal) | Up to 14 days/year | Unpaid |
| 普通傷病假 (Sick) | Up to 30 days/year (non-hospitalized) | Half pay |
| 公傷病假 (Work injury) | As needed for recovery | Full pay |
| 公假 (Civic duty) | As needed | Full pay |

Additional leave types from the Act of Gender Equality in Employment are covered in Section 3 below.

### §50-52 — Maternity-Related (LSA provisions)

- **產假 (Maternity leave)**: 8 weeks before/after delivery; 4 weeks for miscarriage after 3 months
- Pay: full pay if employed 6+ months; half pay if under 6 months
- 安胎休養: follows worker leave regulations for pregnancy complications

## 2. Labor Pension Act

PCode: N0030020

The "new system" (新制) pension regime, applicable to workers who chose the new system after July 1, 2005, and mandatory for all workers hired after that date.

### §6 — Coverage

All workers subject to the LSA are covered, unless they are:
- Self-employed
- Hired by natural persons for domestic work (where no business entity exists)

### §14 — Employer Contribution

- Employer must contribute **no less than 6%** of the worker's monthly wages to the worker's individual pension account at the Bureau of Labor Insurance
- The worker may voluntarily contribute up to **6%** of monthly wages (tax-deductible from gross income)
- Contributions are based on the **Labor Pension Monthly Contribution Wage Classification Table** (勞工退休金月提繳分級表), not the exact salary

### §23-1 — Pension Claim

- Age 60+: may claim pension
- 15+ years of contribution: monthly pension or lump sum
- Under 15 years: lump sum only
- Accumulated contributions + investment returns are portable between employers

### Key Contract Points

- Employment contracts should state that the employer will contribute to labor pension per the Act
- Salary structure should clearly separate base wage from non-wage items to avoid disputes about contribution basis
- Any "salary inclusive of pension contribution" (含勞退) arrangement is illegal — the 6% is the employer's obligation on top of wages

## 3. Act of Gender Equality in Employment

PCode: N0030014

Renamed from 性別工作平等法 to 性別平等工作法 in 2023. Covers workplace gender equality, sexual harassment prevention, and family-friendly leave.

### §7-11 — Anti-Discrimination

Employers cannot discriminate based on sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, or gender expression in:
- Recruitment, hiring, assignment
- Compensation, promotion, demotion
- Training, education, welfare benefits
- Retirement, termination, layoff

### §13 — Sexual Harassment Prevention

Employers with **30+ employees** must establish:
- Written sexual harassment prevention measures
- Complaint and disciplinary procedures
- Regular training

Employers with **10+ employees** must post complaint channels.

All employers must take immediate corrective action upon learning of workplace sexual harassment.

### §15-16 — Family-Related Leave

| Leave type | Duration | Pay | Notes |
|-----------|----------|-----|-------|
| 產假 (Maternity) | 8 weeks | Full (6+ months service) / Half (<6 months) | LSA §50 overlaps |
| 陪產檢及陪產假 (Paternity) | 7 days | Full pay | Renamed and expanded in 2022 amendment |
| 安胎休養假 (Pregnancy rest) | Per medical need | Follows sick leave rules | Doctor's certificate required |
| 育嬰留職停薪 (Parental leave without pay) | Up to 2 years per child (until age 3) | Employment insurance subsidy ~80% of avg monthly salary for 6 months | Both parents eligible |
| 家庭照顧假 (Family care) | 7 days/year | Unpaid (counted as personal leave) | Cannot be grounds for adverse action |

### §21 — Protection Against Adverse Action

Employers cannot refuse leave requests or take adverse employment actions (demotion, dismissal, penalty) against employees exercising rights under this Act. Violations may result in fines and reinstatement orders.

### §38-1 — Penalties

- Discrimination violations: NT$300,000 – NT$1,500,000 fine
- Sexual harassment prevention failures: NT$100,000 – NT$500,000 fine
- Repeated violations: name and shame (公布姓名/名稱)

## 4. Employment Service Act

PCode: N0090001

### §5 — Anti-Discrimination (Broader Scope)

Employers cannot discriminate based on race, class, language, thought, religion, political party, place of origin, birth place, gender, sexual orientation, age, marital status, appearance, facial features, disability, horoscope, blood type, or past union membership.

This list is broader than the Gender Equality Act. Courts and the MOL increasingly enforce this provision.

### §5 para 2 — Job Posting Restrictions

- Job postings cannot state discriminatory preferences (age, gender, appearance, etc.)
- Cannot require non-job-related personal information
- Penalties: NT$300,000 – NT$1,500,000

### §42-52 — Foreign Workers (外國人聘僱)

When an employment contract involves a foreign national worker:
- Employer must obtain a work permit (聘僱許可) from the MOL
- Specific categories: professional/technical work (§46 para 1 items 1-6), blue-collar workers (§46 para 1 items 8-11)
- Professional foreign workers (white-collar): employer applies based on qualifications
- Blue-collar foreign workers: quota system, domestic recruitment priority
- Employment Gold Card (就業金卡): special permit for qualified foreign professionals

Contract review points for foreign worker employment:
- Verify the work permit category matches the job description
- Ensure the salary meets the minimum for the permit category (often higher than general minimum wage for white-collar permits)
- Check duration alignment with work permit validity
- Include language about permit renewal obligations

## 5. Occupational Safety and Health Act

PCode: N0060001

### §5-6 — Employer Duties

Employers must:
- Prevent hazards from machinery, equipment, tools, raw materials, gases, vapor, dust, and other substances
- Provide necessary safety and health equipment and measures
- Conduct worker health protection: general health exams, special health exams for hazardous work

### §20 — Health Examinations

| Type | When | Cost |
|------|------|------|
| 一般體格檢查 (Pre-employment) | Before or at start of employment | Employer pays |
| 一般健康檢查 (Periodic) | Per MOL schedule (annually for <40, every 3 years for 40-65, annually for 65+) | Employer pays |
| 特殊健康檢查 (Special) | For workers in designated hazardous jobs | Employer pays |

Employers cannot require workers to bear health examination costs. Results must be kept confidential and managed per PDPA requirements.

### §37 — Incident Reporting

Employers must report to the labor inspection authority within 8 hours if a workplace accident results in:
- Death of a worker
- Hospitalization of 3+ workers
- Hospitalization of 1+ workers with amputation, blindness, etc.

### Contract Points

- Employment contracts should address workplace safety obligations
- Health examination data triggers PDPA requirements (special personal data — health/medical)
- Consider linking to the personal data consent form for health check data

## 6. Employment Contract Patterns

### Minimum Required Terms

While the LSA does not prescribe a mandatory written format for indefinite-term contracts, the following terms should always be included for legal clarity:

1. **Parties**: employer legal name + business registration number; employee name + ID
2. **Position and duties**: job title, department, reporting line, scope of work
3. **Contract type**: indefinite-term (不定期) or fixed-term (定期) with justification
4. **Start date**: effective date of employment
5. **Place of work**: primary workplace; if relocation is possible, state the scope
6. **Working hours**: daily/weekly hours, flexible working hour system if applicable, overtime consent mechanism
7. **Rest days and holidays**: which day is 例假, which is 休息日
8. **Wages**: base salary (本薪), fixed allowances, calculation method, payment date, payment method
9. **Leave**: reference to LSA §38 annual leave, Worker Leave Regulations, and Gender Equality Act leave
10. **Termination**: grounds, notice periods (§16), severance calculation (§17)
11. **Non-compete**: if included, must comply with §9-1 requirements
12. **Confidentiality**: scope, duration, obligations
13. **Pension**: statement that employer will contribute per Labor Pension Act
14. **Insurance**: labor insurance (勞保) and national health insurance (健保) enrollment
15. **Governing law and jurisdiction**

### Wage Structure Design

Getting the wage structure right is critical because it affects overtime calculation, pension contribution, labor insurance premium, and severance:

| Item | Counted as "wages" (工資)? | Notes |
|------|--------------------------|-------|
| 本薪 (Base salary) | Yes | Always wages |
| 職務加給 (Position allowance) | Yes, if fixed and regular | Part of overtime calculation basis |
| 全勤獎金 (Perfect attendance) | Yes, if regularly paid | MOL interpretation: if paid every month when conditions met, it's wages |
| 績效獎金 (Performance bonus) | Depends on regularity | If paid monthly per formula → wages; if discretionary and irregular → not wages |
| 年終獎金 (Year-end bonus) | Generally no | Unless contractually guaranteed as a fixed amount |
| 伙食津貼 (Meal allowance) | Yes, if fixed and regular | Up to NT$3,000/month may be excluded from taxable income, but still counts as wages for labor law purposes |
| 交通津貼 (Transport allowance) | Yes, if fixed and regular | Same as meal allowance — tax treatment differs from labor law treatment |

### Probation Period (試用期)

- The LSA does not explicitly regulate probation periods, but courts and MOL recognize them as legitimate
- Typical duration: 3 months (some companies use 6 months for senior roles)
- During probation, the employer may terminate with lower threshold than §11/§12, but must still act in good faith and give reasonable notice
- Probation terms should be stated in the contract, including evaluation criteria and consequences of not passing

## 7. Termination, Severance & Non-Compete

### §11 — Employer-Initiated Termination (Economic Reasons)

Employer may terminate with advance notice if:
1. Business closure or transfer
2. Business contraction (虧損或業務緊縮)
3. Force majeure causing business suspension for 1+ month
4. Change of business nature requiring workforce reduction, and no suitable alternative position available
5. Worker clearly unable to perform duties (確不能勝任工作)

Before invoking item 5, the employer must demonstrate that it has provided training, adjustment opportunities, or other support — the "ultima ratio" principle (解僱最後手段性).

### §12 — Employer-Initiated Termination (Cause)

Employer may terminate without notice or severance if the worker:
1. Misrepresented qualifications at hiring
2. Committed violence or serious insult against employer/co-workers/family
3. Been sentenced to imprisonment (not suspended)
4. Seriously violated the employment contract or work rules, making continued employment untenable
5. Intentionally damaged employer's property or leaked trade secrets causing material harm
6. Was absent without leave for 3+ consecutive days, or 6+ days in a month

**30-day rule**: §12 termination must be exercised within 30 days of the employer learning of the cause. Failure to act within this window waives the right.

### §13 — Termination Restrictions

Employer cannot terminate during:
- Occupational injury/illness treatment period + 30 days after
- Maternity leave (before delivery + 8 weeks after)

Exceptions: business closure with approval from competent authority.

### §14 — Employee-Initiated Termination (Cause)

Employee may terminate without notice if:
1. Employer misrepresented working conditions
2. Employer or representative committed violence or serious insult
3. Work endangers health (employer fails to improve after request)
4. Employer violates the labor contract or labor laws, harming employee's rights
5. Employer fails to pay wages on time
6. Employer transfers the employee to unsuitable work without consent (§10-1)

In items 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, the employee is entitled to severance pay as if terminated under §11.

### §16 — Advance Notice Periods

| Service period | Notice required |
|---------------|----------------|
| 3 months – 1 year | 10 days |
| 1 – 3 years | 20 days |
| 3+ years | 30 days |
| Under 3 months | No statutory notice required |

During the notice period, the employee is entitled to 2 hours per work day of paid job-search leave (§16 para 2).

### §17 — Severance Pay (新制 / New System)

For workers under the Labor Pension Act (new system):
- **½ month's average wage per year of service**
- Fractions of a year are prorated
- Max severance: 6 months' average wage

For workers under the old system (LSA §55):
- 2 months' average wage per year of service (first 15 years)
- 1 month per year thereafter
- Max: 45 months

"Average wage" (平均工資) = total wages in the 6 months before termination ÷ total days in that period × 30.

### §9-1 — Non-Compete After Termination

All four conditions must be met for a post-employment non-compete to be enforceable:

1. **Employer has legitimate business interests to protect** (protectable interests): trade secrets, confidential proprietary information, or intellectual property that would be harmed by competition
2. **Employee held a position with access** to such information (not just any employee)
3. **Restrictions are reasonable** in scope:
   - Duration: **max 2 years**
   - Geographic scope: related to the employer's business territory
   - Professional scope: limited to job-related competitive activities
   - Employer activities: related to the employee's actual duties
4. **Reasonable compensation** is provided:
   - Must be at least **50% of the employee's average monthly wage** at termination, paid monthly during the non-compete period
   - Compensation must be adequate considering the employee's lost income potential
   - The compensation is separate from severance pay

**Enforceability consequences**:
- If any of the four conditions is not met, the non-compete clause is **void** (not merely unenforceable or reducible)
- Courts will not "blue pencil" — they invalidate the entire clause
- Unlike consultant non-competes (governed by Civil Code general principles), employee non-competes under §9-1 have a bright-line test

### Termination Process Checklist

When drafting or reviewing termination clauses:

1. Specify which LSA grounds apply (§11 or §12)
2. Reference advance notice periods (§16)
3. State severance calculation method and payment timeline
4. Address return of company property and data
5. Clarify status of accrued but unused annual leave (paid out per §38)
6. If non-compete applies, include all four §9-1 elements in the contract
7. Address confidentiality survival period post-termination
8. Include procedures for termination documentation (離職證明書)
9. Note labor insurance and pension account processing upon termination

## 8. Common Pitfalls Checklist

Use this checklist when reviewing any Taiwan employment contract or labor-related document:

- [ ] Contract clearly states whether it is indefinite-term or fixed-term; if fixed-term, justification for non-continuous work is given
- [ ] Wage structure separates base salary from allowances; identifies which items count as "wages" (工資) for overtime/severance calculation
- [ ] Overtime consent mechanism is specified; overtime rates match §24 minimums
- [ ] Weekly working hours do not exceed 40 hours (or applicable flexible system cap)
- [ ] Monthly overtime does not exceed 46 hours (or 54 hours with proper agreement)
- [ ] Annual leave entitlement matches §38 minimums based on service period
- [ ] Maternity leave is 8 weeks at full pay (for 6+ months service); paternity leave is 7 days
- [ ] Termination clause references specific LSA grounds (§11 or §12), not just "at will"
- [ ] Advance notice periods match §16 minimums
- [ ] Severance pay calculation matches §17 (new system) or §55 (old system)
- [ ] Non-compete clause (if any) includes all four §9-1 elements: protectable interest, qualifying position, reasonable scope, adequate compensation
- [ ] Non-compete duration does not exceed 2 years
- [ ] Non-compete compensation is at least 50% of average monthly wage
- [ ] Probation period terms and evaluation criteria are stated
- [ ] Employer's labor pension contribution (6%+) is stated; no "salary inclusive of pension" language
- [ ] Labor insurance and health insurance enrollment is addressed
- [ ] Sexual harassment prevention measures are referenced (mandatory for 30+ employee firms)
- [ ] Health examination obligations and data handling comply with PDPA
- [ ] Foreign worker provisions (if applicable) align with work permit category and salary requirements
- [ ] R.O.C. calendar date format used
- [ ] Governing law and jurisdiction specified
